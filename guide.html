Two phones, one little place

Sign in with your own account. Your side stays yours; shared things show up for both of you. The bottom row is home, list, add, today, and more; notes live at the top of More, and unread ones show up in Next up on Home. The magnifying glass beside each page title searches across your stuff.

⌂Home & the fridge＋

The sky shows both of you at a glance. Tap your own character for a quick status; tap your partner’s to see their side without getting their controls. “Next up” shows the first few things Today lists in full, plus unread notes, today’s question and what’s new. Finish a due task or answer an ask right there; titles still open the full item.

Tap the little fridge note to pin a short message on both home screens. A note from Notes can go there too; only one note is pinned at a time.

go home →
＋Add & search＋

The middle add button opens the right form for the page you’re on. On Home, Today, Right now, Settings, or this guide, it lets you choose a to-do, grocery item, note, ask, or date idea. Search (⌕) finds things across the app and opens the matching item.

If you delete something by accident, tap undo before the little bar disappears.

◎Today, questions & focus＋

Today lets you check off due tasks and answer asks without leaving. A new couple question opens at 8 a.m. Eastern. Neither of you can read the other answer until both have answered; then it folds to one line with “see answers.” Questions don’t repeat within this batch.

The focus button starts a short timer and puts it in your status. “What’s new” shows your partner’s recent actions, not your own or automatic online changes; its menu can remove an item for you or both of you.

open Today →
✓The list, groceries & asks＋

To do is the shared checklist. Add a day or repeat schedule under “when / repeat”; tap an item to edit it, check it off when done, or swipe right to finish. Groceries keeps items by aisle and lets you bring back a recent grab.

Asks are requests for the other person (or a reminder to yourself). Pick a time if it needs to ring later. Answer an incoming ask with “on it,” “in a bit,” or “can’t rn.” Take back one of yours with “never mind”, with a moment to undo.

open the list →
💌Notes & reactions＋

Write in the little box at the bottom of Notes and press ↑ to send. For a starter or mood icon, use the middle add button. Older notes are under show more.

Tap the little ♡ to react with any emoji, then tap your reaction to change or remove it. Notes can be pinned to the fridge. Your own note can be edited or deleted from its menu.

open Notes →
☀︎☾Right now & the map＋

Tap your own card to edit whether you’re around, away, busy, or lurking, and to add your own words. The quick statuses on your card (busy, home soon, out, leaving now, almost there, focus) are the same ones you get by tapping yourself on Home. Your partner’s status is for looking, not editing.

Location is opt-in. The map appears once there’s a live or last-known spot to show; you can pause sharing or remove your last spot. Saved spots now live in Settings and can change your status when you arrive. A website cannot keep tracking after it’s closed or the phone locks.

open Right now →
✦Date ideas＋

Save ideas with a vibe and optional details. Use the little decision machine to filter by vibe or budget and pick one at random. “Let’s do it” puts the idea on the list for a day you choose. Mark a date done when it happens; it can join your memories.

open Date ideas →
◒Memories＋

Save a few words and, if you want, a photo. Tap a memory to open it larger, or “pull one out” to revisit a random one. Delete has an undo window.

open Memories →
⚙︎Settings & phone pings＋

Settings opens with your names. The phone check sits at the bottom, folded, and only opens itself when something needs a look. Change your names, pings, quiet hours, sound, and vibration there; changes save on their own. Saved spots live here too.

On iPhone, add the website to the Home Screen before asking for push notifications. Android can install it too. Phone settings still have the final say over sound and vibration. The site can open offline and sync again when connected, but live location needs the page open.

open Settings →
