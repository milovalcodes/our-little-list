Two phones, one little place

Sign in with your own account. Your side stays yours; shared things show up for both of you. The bottom row is home, list, add, notes, and more. The magnifying glass at the top searches across your stuff.

⌂Home & the fridge＋

The sky shows both of you at a glance. Tap your own character for a quick status; tap your partner’s to see their side without getting their controls. “Next up” opens whatever needs attention.

Tap the little fridge note to pin a short message on both home screens. A note from Notes can go there too; only one note is pinned at a time.

go home →
＋Add & search＋

The middle add button can make a to-do, grocery item, note, ask, or date idea. The pages also have their own add buttons if you’re already there. Search (⌕) finds things across the app.

If you delete something by accident, tap undo before the little bar disappears.

◎Today, questions & focus＋

Today shows what’s due or waiting on you. A new couple question opens at 8 a.m. Eastern. Answer on your own phone: neither of you can read the other answer until you’ve both answered. When one answers, the other gets a nudge; once both answer, you can compare and edit your own answer. Questions don’t repeat within this batch.

The focus button starts a short timer and puts it in your status. “What’s new” is the recent activity feed; mark it seen or use an item’s menu to remove it for yourself or both of you.

open Today →
✓The list, groceries & asks＋

To do is the shared checklist. Add a day or repeat schedule under “when / repeat”; tap an item to edit it, check it off when done, or swipe right to finish. Groceries keeps items by aisle and lets you bring back a recent grab.

Asks are requests for the other person (or a reminder to yourself). Pick a time if it needs to ring later. Answer an incoming ask with “on it,” “in a bit,” or “can’t rn.” Swipe left or use the item menu to delete, with a moment to undo.

open the list →
💌Notes & reactions＋

Send a short note from Notes or quick add. Pick a starter or type your own, then choose its tiny mood icon. Your partner gets a ping if their phone allows it.

Tap react to use any emoji, then tap your reaction to change or remove it. Notes can be pinned to the fridge. Your own note can be edited for a while or deleted from its menu.

open Notes →
☀︎☾Right now & the map＋

Set whether you’re around, away, busy, or lurking, and add your own words: music, food, a thought, whatever. Quick updates like “leaving now” are one tap. Your partner’s status is for looking, not editing.

Location is opt-in. You can turn it on, pause it, or remove your last spot. The map shows live or last-known spots and can expand when you want a closer look. Saved spots can label places and change your status when you arrive; arrival and leaving pings are optional. A website cannot keep tracking after it’s closed or the phone locks.

open Right now →
✦Date ideas＋

Save ideas with a vibe and optional details. Use the little decision machine to filter by vibe or budget and pick one at random. “Let’s do it” puts the idea on the list for a day you choose. Mark a date done when it happens; it can join your memories.

open Date ideas →
◒Memories＋

Save a few words and, if you want, a photo. Tap a memory to open it larger, or “pull one out” to revisit a random one. Delete has an undo window.

open Memories →
⚙︎Settings & phone pings＋

Settings checks sync, internet, installation, notification permission, and location permission. The buttons there ask the phone for what a website is allowed to request. You can change your names, which pings you want, quiet hours, in-app sound, and vibration.

On iPhone, add the website to the Home Screen before asking for push notifications. Android can install it too. Phone settings still have the final say over sound and vibration. The site can open offline and sync again when connected, but live location needs the page open.

open Settings →
