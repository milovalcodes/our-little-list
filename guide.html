Guide

start here

Two phones, one little place

Sign in with your own account. Your side stays yours; shared things show up for both of you. The bottom row is home, list, activities, today, and more; notes live at the top of More, and unread ones show up in Next up on Home. The magnifying glass beside each page title searches across your stuff.

⌂Home & the fridge＋

Home and Today are now one page: both characters, due tasks and routines, a focus button, the fridge note, and folded Updates. Finish tasks here or open their title to edit. Notes and games have their own bottom-bar buttons instead of repeating their content on Home or profiles.

A small marker above a destination means something needs you: unread notes, a new partner task or due routine, unfinished daily activities, or new profiles, dates and memories inside More. October markers are bright orange pumpkins; December uses a golden seasonal marker, and other months a dot. Reading Notes clears its badge; finishing routines or games clears those. Visiting a section acknowledges its new items. Automatic online changes do not add badges.

Tap the little fridge note to pin a short message on both home screens. A note from Notes can go there too; only one note is pinned at a time.

Portraits, buttons, map pins, notification artwork and little bits of wording dress up for October and December, using Eastern time. The usual look returns for the other months. Your chosen emojis and photos stay yours.

The Home Screen icon gets seasonal artwork too. Android may take a while to refresh it; iPhone may keep the icon from when you added the app. That does not stop the in-app theme or notifications from updating. Re-adding on iPhone may require signing in and enabling notifications again.

＋Add & search＋

The small add button at the top of the list, dates and memories opens that page’s form. Home’s add button offers a to-do, grocery item, note or date idea, plus a shortcut to routines. Notes has its own writing bar. Search (⌕) finds things across the app and opens the matching item.

If you delete something by accident, tap undo before the little bar disappears.

◎Focus & updates＋

The Today section is part of Home, not another destination. Older Today links open the same Home page.

The focus button starts a short timer and puts it in your status. The folded Updates section keeps a short history of partner actions. Notes stay in Notes and are not marked read from Home. The update menu can remove an item for you or both of you.

✓The list & routines＋

To do is the shared checklist; Groceries groups your shopping by aisle. Tap a title to edit, check it off, or swipe right to finish. Deleted rows offer undo.

Routines: choose the weekdays when adding a routine. Either of you can check it off or undo it. It stays checked for that day and resets at midnight Eastern time, including when you return after being away. Off-day routines remain visible but cannot be checked.

Reminders: open the extra options on a task or routine, choose a time (Eastern), who gets notified, and any of 1 hour, 30 minutes, 15 minutes before, or when due. Completed or deleted items stop their pending reminders. These are push reminders, not phone alarms; phone permissions, connection and system settings still apply.

Nudge / need a hand? sends the other person a notification linked to that exact task. Asks and separate alarms are retired; existing asks remain as checklist items, with their original scheduled notifications preserved.

open the list →
💌Notes & reactions＋

Write in the little box at the bottom of Notes and press ↑ to send. Tap the mood icon to change it; tap the writing bar for quick starters. Older notes are under show more. Notes count as read when they appear on screen, not just because the page is open in another tab.

Tap the little ♡ to react with any emoji, then tap your reaction to change or remove it. Notes can be pinned to the fridge. Your own note can be edited or deleted from its menu.

open Notes →
☀︎☾Profiles & the map＋

Tap your own card to edit whether you’re around, away, busy, or lurking, and to add your own words. The quick statuses live here too: busy, home soon, out, leaving now, almost there, and focus. Use the two profile tabs to look at either person; viewing never changes your account. Your partner’s status is for looking, not editing.

Your profile photo: tap the camera badge on your own portrait to add a photo, check the square preview, then save. Once you have one, the camera opens Change photo and Remove photo. Closing the preview without saving leaves your current photo alone. If a photo fails to open or save, the editor shows the problem and keeps you there to try again; a sync failure has a Retry connection button. With a photo uploaded, tap a Home portrait once to flip to it; double tap the portrait to open that person’s profile. Without a photo, one tap opens the profile. The name below the portrait is always a direct profile link, including for keyboard users. On a profile, tap the portrait to peek and tap again to flip back; it returns automatically after a moment. Only you can replace or remove your photo; both of you can see it. Photos stay private and never replace the seasonal characters or app icon.

Location is opt-in. The map appears once there’s a live or last-known spot to show; you can pause sharing or remove your last spot. Saved spots now live in Settings and can change your status when you arrive.

On the way to each other, the other phone hears about it: leaving work (or school), almost together within about 1.5 km, and together at last when you meet. A website can only see where you are while it’s open on screen, so for live pings tap 🚗 on my way on your profile (or the leaving now status): it keeps the screen on, shows the distance left, and switches off once you’re together. If it was closed, the next time you open it still tells them, worded as news from a little while ago (up to 3 hours).

✦Date ideas＋

Save ideas with a vibe and optional details. Use the little decision machine to filter by vibe or budget and pick one at random. “Let’s do it” puts the idea on the list for a day you choose. Mark a date done to save it in your memories. Finishing or undoing a date needs a connection: both records change together, even if you both tap done.

◒Memories＋

Your memories: add a description, optional photo, and the date it happened—even years ago. Memories sort by that date, not upload time. Existing entries keep their original dates.

App memories: past daily questions, completed dates, and weekly coronations live here. A settled crown appears once, using the saved weekly result: your sun was crowned queen, or your moon was crowned king, with their profile name and the actual crown date. Open it to revisit the weekly recap. Historic wins appear automatically; an unknown old date is labelled by week rather than invented. Questions keep the same private-answer rules.

Save a few words and, if you want, a photo. Tap a memory to open it larger, or “pull one out” to revisit a random one. Delete has an undo window. Past daily questions with at least one answer appear here automatically. Tap one to revisit the answers; the original privacy rules still apply, and unanswered questions don’t fill the jar.

open Memories →
✎Practice makes perfect＋

Open Activities and pick a game under Practice makes perfect. The number beside each button is your current puzzle out of the whole collection; the smaller count is how many you have finished. There are 200 Little Words, 100 Word Searches and 100 mini crosswords. Practice is a separate, assorted year-round collection, not the daily answer schedule.

Little Word: five tries, with a clue button that first gives a definition, then the first letter, then two more letters. Unrecognized and repeated guesses never cost a try. Run out? Start the puzzle over or move on. Word Search and crossword: the same controls as the daily games, without a timer. Crosswords show their clues; select one, type an answer and check. Word Search supports dragging, tapping the endpoints, or arrow keys and Enter.

Use next puzzle or the numbered picker to move around. Unfinished boards stay saved. Restart affects only that board; your finished count remains. After the last puzzle, the collection loops for unlimited replay. There are no scores, daily deadlines, partner pings or crowns here. Progress stays on this browser, separately for each side, and is not synced across devices. If browser storage is blocked or full, a warning explains why progress cannot be kept. Additional accepted guesses are from a MIT-licensed list; practice clues are original.

☀︎☾Activities · the daily four＋

The daily question and three puzzles open at 9 a.m. Eastern and stay until the next 9 a.m. One opening notification covers all four. Our question: neither answer is visible to the other person until both answer. Answered questions move into Memories at the next reset; a single answer stays sealed to the partner. The question batch does not repeat.

Little Word: guess the same five-letter word in five attempts, on your own boards. Once you both finish (solved or out of tries), peek their process reveals your partner’s guesses in order, with the same letter colours. Their unfinished board stays private; the button appears live when you’re both done. The legend identifies right-place and misplaced letters. Letters absent from the answer turn dark on the board and keyboard; untried keys keep their normal colour. A repeated letter only lights up as many times as it occurs in the answer. Unrecognized or repeated guesses use no attempt. Your progress is saved across your screens; reconnect to submit. Partner guesses stay private. First-try wins get the biggest confetti, second-try wins get a smaller celebration, and tries 3–5 get a little heart. A miss gets a floating sad face. These play only after your saved final guess, not every time you open the board.

Word Search & Mini crossword: each gives you two minutes from tapping start. Find words by dragging or tapping the first and last letter in Word Search. In the crossword, select a clue, type its answer and tap check. Wrong answers cost time, not points. Score = words found ÷ words on the board × 50, rounded to a whole point. Nothing found earns 0; a full board earns 50. Sunday uses harder words and doubles the rounded score. Timers keep running through refreshes or leaving the page; only confirmed, saved words count. Your routes reveal after both timers end or both boards finish.

Puzzle catalog: theme hints appear above each board. Normal and hard word-and-clue pools are separate. Saved daily puzzle records retain their words, theme and catalog version when more content is added. Boards vary by day; individual clue words can return in new combinations. October uses Halloween, horror and fall pools; December uses Christmas and winter pools. These themed pools are only selected in their month, using Eastern time. Other months use the regular catalog. Sunday and tie-break rounds still use hard pools. Already-open rounds keep their original theme until they close, including across a month boundary. Little Word uses separate seasonal answer banks alongside its regular bank and larger accepted-guess dictionary. The new games begin counting from Oct 9; earlier puzzles are not invented retroactively. Existing Little Word scores are shown at 10× their old scale, without changing winners.

Weekly crown: a win on tries 1–5 earns 100 / 40 / 30 / 20 / 10 points; missing earns 0. Sunday uses a harder bank and doubles those points. The week runs Monday–Sunday. Scores settle once both finish all three Sunday rounds, or Monday at 9 a.m. A tied week opens one new hard round of each of the three games, with double points. Points accumulate across sets; another tie opens another fresh set. The winner is crowned only when the other person cannot win or tie even with their remaining maximum points. Little Word has no timer; each Word Search and crossword still lasts two minutes once started. The winner wears a crown above their avatars. Consecutive weekly wins add a small number and a little size; the old crown stays while a tie-break is unresolved.

The coronation: once a winner is settled, the final scores count up and their character gets crowned, with confetti and their winning streak. It appears once per person on this browser, including when you next open the app within a week of the result. It waits while you are typing, playing a timed round, or using another sheet. Skip it at any time; reduced-motion mode shows the result without the moving effects.

Weekly tracker: open The weekly race at the top of Activities. The big totals include all three games and any tie-break rounds. Each game has its own point tally; tap a day to see how many guesses or words earned each score. Use the week picker to revisit a finished week. Recaps show your biggest source of points, your best scoring rate (earned points as a percentage of the maximum for finished rounds), and the winner’s biggest per-game advantage. Old missing details are labelled rather than invented. A crown notification opens that exact week. The question stays separate and does not award points.

The old play-together games are retired. Their shortcuts and pings are gone; old links return to daily Activities, and past records are kept.

open Activities →
⚙︎Settings & phone pings＋

Settings opens with your names. The phone check sits at the bottom, folded, and only opens itself when something needs a look. Change your names, pings, quiet hours, sound, and vibration there; changes save on their own. Saved spots live here too.

On iPhone, add the website to the Home Screen before asking for push notifications. Android can install it too. Tap a ping to open its item or person, even if that page is already open. Daily activities and list reminders have their own switches. Phone settings still have the final say over sound and vibration. The site can open offline and sync again when connected, but live location needs the page open.
