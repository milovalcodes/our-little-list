Two phones, one little place

Sign in with your own account. Your side stays yours; shared things show up for both of you. The bottom row is home, list, games, today, and more; notes live at the top of More, and unread ones show up in Next up on Home. The magnifying glass beside each page title searches across your stuff.

⌂Home & the fridge＋

The sky shows both of you at a glance. Tap either character to open their profile. Yours has your controls; your partner’s has their status, recent notes, and buttons to leave a note or ask for a hand. “Next up” shows the first few things Today lists in full, plus unread notes, today’s question and what’s new. Finish a due task or answer an ask right there; titles still open the full item.

Tap the little fridge note to pin a short message on both home screens. A note from Notes can go there too; only one note is pinned at a time.

Portraits, buttons, map pins, notification artwork and little bits of wording dress up for October and December, using Eastern time. The usual look returns for the other months. Your chosen emojis and photos stay yours.

The Home Screen icon gets seasonal artwork too. Android may take a while to refresh it; iPhone may keep the icon from when you added the app. That does not stop the in-app theme or notifications from updating. Re-adding on iPhone may require signing in and enabling notifications again.

＋Add & search＋

The small add button at the top of the list, dates and memories opens that page’s form. Home’s add button offers a to-do, grocery item, note, ask or date idea. Notes has its own writing bar. Search (⌕) finds things across the app and opens the matching item.

If you delete something by accident, tap undo before the little bar disappears.

◎Today, questions & focus＋

Today lets you check off due tasks and answer asks without leaving. A new couple question opens at 8 a.m. Eastern. Neither of you can read the other answer until both have answered; then it folds to one line with “see answers.” Questions don’t repeat within this batch.

The focus button starts a short timer and puts it in your status. “What’s new” shows your partner’s recent actions, not your own or automatic online changes; its menu can remove an item for you or both of you.

open Today →
✓The list, groceries & asks＋

To do is the shared checklist. Add a day or repeat schedule under “when / repeat”; tap an item to edit it, check it off when done, or swipe right to finish. Groceries keeps items by aisle and lets you bring back a recent grab.

Asks are requests for the other person (or a reminder to yourself). Pick a time if it needs to ring later. Answer an incoming ask with “on it,” “in a bit,” or “can’t rn.” Take back one of yours with “never mind”, with a moment to undo.

open the list →
💌Notes & reactions＋

Write in the little box at the bottom of Notes and press ↑ to send. Tap the mood icon to change it; tap the writing bar for quick starters. Older notes are under show more. Notes count as read when they appear on screen, not just because the page is open in another tab.

Tap the little ♡ to react with any emoji, then tap your reaction to change or remove it. Notes can be pinned to the fridge. Your own note can be edited or deleted from its menu.

open Notes →
☀︎☾Profiles & the map＋

Tap your own card to edit whether you’re around, away, busy, or lurking, and to add your own words. The quick statuses live here too: busy, home soon, out, leaving now, almost there, and focus. Use the two profile tabs to look at either person; viewing never changes your account. Your partner’s status is for looking, not editing.

Location is opt-in. The map appears once there’s a live or last-known spot to show; you can pause sharing or remove your last spot. Saved spots now live in Settings and can change your status when you arrive.

On the way to each other, the other phone hears about it: leaving work (or school), almost together within about 1.5 km, and together at last when you meet. A website can only see where you are while it’s open on screen, so for live pings tap 🚗 on my way on your profile (or the leaving now status): it keeps the screen on, shows the distance left, and switches off once you’re together. If it was closed, the next time you open it still tells them, worded as news from a little while ago (up to 3 hours).

open Profiles →
✦Date ideas＋

Save ideas with a vibe and optional details. Use the little decision machine to filter by vibe or budget and pick one at random. “Let’s do it” puts the idea on the list for a day you choose. Mark a date done to save it in your memories. Finishing or undoing a date needs a connection: both records change together, even if you both tap done.

open Date ideas →
◒Memories＋

Save a few words and, if you want, a photo. Tap a memory to open it larger, or “pull one out” to revisit a random one. Delete has an undo window.

open Memories →
☀︎☾Sun vs Moon · games＋

Tap games in the bottom row. Pick a game from the shelf, then invite your partner: they get the first move. Use “all games” to return to the shelf without ending your round. Rules, match totals and ending a round live in “rules & round options.” Each game keeps its own board, round score and match wins. First to 3 rounds wins a match; a rematch resets the round score, not your match wins. You can switch games without losing a board.

Three to Move: place 3 pieces each, then move one of yours to any empty square. Get 3 in a row across, down or diagonally. A round draws after 30 moves.

Four in a Row: tap a column to drop your piece into its lowest free space. Connect 4 across, down or diagonally. A full board with no line is a draw.

Dots & Boxes: tap a line between two dots. Finish a box to claim it and take another turn. Once all 9 boxes are filled, whoever owns more wins. One line can claim two boxes. Your bonus turns stay yours; the other phone gets a turn ping only when it’s actually their turn.

Play on separate phones, at your own pace. Every ongoing game stays in Next up on Home, whether it’s your turn or theirs. Tap it to return to that board. The Games shelf shows whose turn it is for every ongoing game. Moves appear automatically while you’re on the page; returning to the app or reconnecting catches the board up without a refresh. A notification opens the right game and round. Background game pings are checked once a minute and respect your game switch, focus timer and quiet hours. Moves need a connection so neither phone can overwrite the other. Either of you can put a round away without awarding a point. Old pings explain when the board has moved on.

pick a game →
⚙︎Settings & phone pings＋

Settings opens with your names. The phone check sits at the bottom, folded, and only opens itself when something needs a look. Change your names, pings, quiet hours, sound, and vibration there; changes save on their own. Saved spots live here too.

On iPhone, add the website to the Home Screen before asking for push notifications. Android can install it too. Tap a ping to open its item or person, even if that page is already open. Game invites and turns have their own switch. Phone settings still have the final say over sound and vibration. The site can open offline and sync again when connected, but live location needs the page open.

open Settings →
